Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

falcon-music-analytics ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of Falcon Brothers Records to manage the label's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to read:

  • channel and video information of the owner's own channels;
  • video analytics — views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, geography, devices and estimated revenue;
  • public comments on the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

  • To produce analytics reports for the channel owner.
  • To draft replies to viewer comments and suggestions for video titles, descriptions and tags. Drafts are sent only to the owner for review.
  • Only after the owner explicitly approves a specific item does the app post that reply or update that video's details on the owner's own channel.

The app never uploads videos, never sells data, never uses data for advertising and never shares data with third parties.

AI drafting

To draft a reply, the text of a public comment and the video title may be sent to an AI language model service used by the owner. This is done solely to prepare a draft for the owner's review; the content is not used for any other purpose by the app.

Storage and retention

Data and OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app does not run a server and does not transfer the data to one. The owner can delete the stored data at any time; analytics snapshots are kept only as long as the owner needs them.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer read or change anything.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
