Who may use the app

falcon-music-analytics is a private tool used exclusively by the owner of Falcon Brothers Records to manage the label's own YouTube channels. It is not available to other users.

How it acts

The app reads analytics and comments of the owner's channels and prepares drafts. It performs a public action — posting a reply or updating a video's title, description or tags — only after the owner approves that exact item. It never uploads videos.

YouTube terms

Using the app means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. See also our Privacy Policy.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, without any warranty, for the owner's own use.
