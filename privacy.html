Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

falcon-music-analytics ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of Falcon Brothers Records to manage the label's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to read: channel and video information, video analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, geography, devices, estimated revenue), and public comments on the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

  • To produce analytics reports for the channel owner.
  • To draft replies to viewer comments and suggestions for video titles, descriptions and tags. Drafts are sent only to the owner for review.
  • Only after the owner explicitly approves an item does the app post that reply or update that video's metadata on the owner's own channel.

Comment texts may be sent to an AI language model solely to draft a reply for the owner's review. The app does not upload videos, does not sell data, does not use data for advertising, and does not share data with third parties.

Storage and retention

Data and OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are not transferred to any server operated by the app. The owner can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
