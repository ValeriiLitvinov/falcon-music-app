falcon-music-analytics

The label's channel tool

falcon-music-analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of Falcon Brothers Records to look after the label's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

It helps the owner understand what listeners enjoy and keeps the conversation with them alive — while every public action stays in the owner's hands.

Read the privacy policy · Terms of service

What it does, in plain words

  • Reads analytics of the label's own channels and videos.
  • Reads comments on the label's own videos.
  • Drafts replies and title / description / tag suggestions for the owner to review.
  • Posts a reply or updates a video's details only after the owner approves that exact item.
  • Never uploads videos, never sells or shares data.
