falcon-music-analytics

falcon-music-analytics is a private, single-owner tool of the independent music label Falcon Brothers Records. It is used only by the label owner to manage the label's own YouTube channels (Marek Vale, Makar Route, Falcon Mind, LEL & BOL).

With the owner's permission it reads channel and video analytics, reads comments on the owner's own videos, drafts replies and metadata improvements, and — only after the owner approves each item — posts those replies or updates the titles, descriptions and tags of the owner's own videos. It does not upload videos and is not offered to other users.
